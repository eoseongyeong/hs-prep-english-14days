문법 1. 상관접속사

앞뒤 문법 구조가 대칭이어야 한다.

상관접속의미
both A and BA와 B 모두
not only A but also BA뿐 아니라 B도
either A or BA 또는 B
neither A nor BA도 B도 아닌
whether A or BA이든 B이든
not A but BA가 아니라 B
Not only did she condemn the act, but she also proposed reforms.
Either you comply with the rules or you face penalties.

연습 1

  1. __________ the evidence __________ the testimony supports the claim.
  2. __________ did he __________ the plan, but he also implemented it.
  3. She is __________ talented __________ diligent.
  4. __________ you accept the offer __________ decline it, notify us by Friday.
정답1 Both / and · 2 Not only / endorse(approve) · 3 not only / but also · 4 Whether / or

문법 2. 비교 구문 심화

원급: as + 형/부 + as · not so/as … as

비교급: -er / more … than · the -er, the -er

최상급: the -est / the most … · one of the + 최상급 + 복수명사

수식어 위치:

Much more significant than expected.
By far the most controversial issue.

비교 대상 일치: Her research is more rigorous than that of her peers. (❌ than her peers)

the + 비교급, the + 비교급: The more you read, the better you write.

연습 2

  1. This policy is __________ (significant) than the previous one.
  2. She is __________ (talented) student in the cohort.
  3. The __________ (hard) you work, the __________ (likely) you succeed.
  4. His argument is as __________ (compelling) as __________ of the lead author.
