단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. abide — (a) 삼가다 (b) 수용하다 (c) 따르다; 견디다
  2. accommodate — (a) 따르다; 견디다 (b) 수용하다 (c) 제출하다
  3. authorize — (a) 위반하다 (b) 의무화하다; 명령 (c) 승인하다
  4. compel — (a) 필요하게 하다 (b) 강요하다 (c) 요구하다

문법 1. 조동사 추측·의무

조동사추측(현재)추측(과거)의무

연습 1

  1. The door is open; someone __________ (must / enter) while we were out.
  2. You __________ (should / notify) us before the deadline.
  3. She __________ (can't / finish) the report — it's only been an hour.
  4. Citizens __________ (oblige) to abide by constitutional law.
정답1 must have entered · 2 should have notified · 3 can't have finished · 4 are obliged

문법 2. 수동태 확장 (4~5형식·지각·사역)

4형식 수동: 간접목적어 → 주어

They gave me a chance. → I was given a chance.

5형식 수동: 목적어 + 보어 → 주어 + be p.p. + 보어

People consider him honest. → He is considered honest.

지각동사 수동: see/hear/watch + O + V → O + be seen + to V

He was seen to enter the building.

사역동사: make + O + V → O + be made to V

She was made to testify.

연습 2

  1. The committee __________ (allocate) funds to priority projects last week.
  2. He __________ (consider) the most reliable witness.
  3. The suspect __________ (see) to leave through the rear exit.
  4. Workers __________ (require) to submit safety reports monthly.
정답1 was allocated · 2 is considered · 3 was seen · 4 are required

문법 3. 준조동사 · that절 should

준조동사 + that + 주어 + (should) + 원형

insist / suggest / recommend / demand / propose / require / urge / mandate …

The law requires that every citizen (should) pay taxes.
I insist that he (should) be present.

should 생략 가능(미국·격식 영어)

suggest + -ing / suggest + that (that절에서 ❌ suggest him to go)

연습 3

  1. The board __________ (recommend) that the CEO __________ (resign).
  2. Regulations __________ (stipulate) that data __________ (protect).
  3. The judge __________ (urge) that witnesses __________ (refrain) from speculation.
  4. It is essential that every applicant __________ (submit) transcripts.
정답1 recommended / (should) resign · 2 stipulate / (should) be protected · 3 urged / (should) refrain · 4 (should) submit
