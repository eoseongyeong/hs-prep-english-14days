단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. attempt — (a) 시도하다; 시도 (b) 선호하다 (c) 두려워하다; 두려움
  2. cease — (a) 중단하다 (b) 피하다 (c) 시도하다; 시도
  3. contemplate — (a) 숙고하다 (b) 노력하다; 노력 (c) 서두르다; 촉진하다
  4. dread — (a) 기억하다;召回하다 (b) 옹호하다; 옹호자 (c) 두려워하다; 두려움

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. Scientists __________ to minimize bias. (노력하다)
  2. Technology __________s remote collaboration. (촉진하다)
  3. Regulations __________ unauthorized access. (금지하다)
  4. The policy __________s to reduce emissions. (의도하다)
정답 보기A: 1-(a) 2-(a) 3-(a) 4-(c)
B: 5. endeavor · 6. facilitate · 7. forbid · 8. intend

문법 1. to V vs V-ing 의미 차

동사to VV-ing
remember미래 기억과거 기억
forget미래 잊음과거 잊음
regret~할 것을 후회~한 것을 후회
try~하려 시도시도·실험
mean~하려 함~을 의미
stop~하기 위해 멈춤~하던 것을 멈춤
I regret to inform you… / I regret informing him.
He tried to open the door. / He tried using another key.

연습 1

  1. She __________ (remember / lock) the door before leaving.
  2. I __________ (regret / say) that the application was rejected.
  3. They __________ (mean / propose) a complete policy overhaul.
  4. He stopped __________ (smoke) to improve his health.
정답1 remembered to lock · 2 regret to say · 3 mean to propose · 4 smoking

문법 2. 동명사·부정사 결합

동명사만: enjoy, avoid, deny, risk, postpone, quit, resist, tolerate, merit …

to부정사만: attempt, endeavor, decide, intend, propose, refuse, venture …

둘 다(의미 같음): like, love, hate, prefer, begin, start, continue …

둘 다(의미 다름): remember, forget, regret, try, mean, stop …

전치사 + V-ing: insist on going · refrain from commenting · object to being …

It is + adj + to V / V-ing: It is risky to venture … / It is no use trying …

연습 2

  1. He denied __________ (omit) critical data from the report.
  2. The plan merits __________ (consider) by the full committee.
  3. She objected to __________ (waive) her contractual rights.
  4. It is difficult __________ (resist) the temptation to speculate.
정답1 omitting · 2 consideration / being considered · 3 waiving · 4 to resist

문법 3. 관계사 심화 (what / whatever / whoever)

what = the thing(s) which · 선행사 포함

What he said surprised everyone.

whatever / whoever / whichever = anything/anyone that

Whatever decision you make, support it.
Whoever violates the rule will be penalized.

the way (that/in which) · the reason (that/why) · the time (that/when)

This is the way (that/in which) we facilitate learning.

연습 3

  1. __________ he proposed was rejected by the board.
  2. __________ attempts to breach security will be prosecuted.
  3. That is the reason __________ the meeting was postponed.
  4. I will support __________ candidate you recommend.
정답1 What · 2 Whoever / Anyone who · 3 why / that · 4 whichever / whatever
