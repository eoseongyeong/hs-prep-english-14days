단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. paraphrase — (a) 그래서 (b) 바꿔 말하다, 다른 말로 표현하다; 바꿔 쓰기 (c) 나란히 놓다, 병치하다
  2. contextualize — (a) 맥락 속에 놓다, 맥락을 부여하다 (b) 따라서 (c) 추론
  3. critique — (a) 전환· 연결 (b) 비평하다; 비평 (c) 개요
  4. juxtapose — (a) 나란히 놓다, 병치하다 (b) 담론 (c) 그러나

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. Please __________ the issue carefully. (일반화하다)
  2. Regulators __________ bank mergers. (면밀히 조사하다)
  3. The evidence is thin; __________, the jury convicted. (그럼에도)
  4. The plan is costly; __________, it lacks public support. (게다가)
정답 보기A: 1-(b) 2-(a) 3-(b) 4-(a)
B: 5. generalize · 6. scrutinize · 7. nevertheless · 8. moreover

문법 1. 독해 신호어 (Transition Words)

기능신호어
추가moreover, furthermore, in addition, likewise
대조however, nevertheless, nonetheless, conversely
원인because, since, owing to, due to
결과therefore, thus, hence, consequently, as a result
예시for instance, such as, namely
양보although, even though, despite, in spite of
결론in conclusion, to sum up, overall

연습 1

  1. The evidence is weak; __________, the assertion remains popular.
  2. Renewable costs fell; __________, adoption accelerated.
  3. __________, the author critiques three competing theories.
  4. Many factors matter; __________, funding and governance stand out.
정답1 however / nevertheless · 2 therefore / consequently · 3 In this excerpt / For instance · 4 for example / namely

문법 2. 문장 압축 · 바꿔쓰기

긴 문 → 짧게

1) 부사절 → 분사구문

Because he was tired → Being tired …

2) 관계절 → 분사/전치사구

students who study hard → students studying hard

3) so…that / too…to / enough to

so complex that … → too complex to …

4) 명사화

The committee decided → The committee's decision

연습 2

  1. Because the data were incomplete, researchers postponed publication. → __________ incomplete, researchers postponed publication.
  2. The argument that he presented was compelling. → The argument __________ __________ was compelling.
  3. The passage is so dense that beginners cannot parse it. → The passage is too dense __________ __________ beginners.
  4. Although the rhetoric was polished, the logic was flawed. → __________ polished rhetoric, the logic was flawed.
정답1 The data being / With the data being · 2 (that) he presented · 3 for / to parse · 4 Despite / Notwithstanding
