단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. absorb — (a) 흡수하다; 몰두하다 (b) 보간하다 (c) 부정·무효화하다
  2. synthesize — (a) 보간하다 (b) 종합하다 (c) 검증하다
  3. hypothesize — (a) 타당성을 확인하다 (b) 가설을 세우다 (c) 분류하다
  4. corroborate — (a) 뒷받침하다 (b) 식별·파악하다 (c) 검증하다

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. Data __________ the central claim. (입증하다)
  2. Peer review __________s scientific findings. (타당성을 확인하다)
  3. Auditors __________ financial statements. (검증하다)
  4. New experiments __________ the old model. (반박하다)
정답 보기A: 1-(a) 2-(b) 3-(b) 4-(a)
B: 5. substantiate · 6. validate · 7. verify · 8. refute

문법 1. 현재완료 신호어 심화

have/has + p.p.

신호의미예
already, yet, just, ever, never경험·완료She has just published a paper.
for / since계속He has researched here since 2018.
so far, up to now, until now지금까지So far, results have validated the model.
recently, lately최근Markets have fluctuated recently.
this week/month/year포함 현재We have compiled three reports this month.

❌ I have met her two years ago. → I met her two years ago.

연습 1

  1. Researchers __________ (already / corroborate) the initial findings.
  2. __________ you ever __________ (hypothesize) about such a link?
  3. The team __________ (investigate) the case for six months.
  4. So far, no study __________ (refute) the central claim.
정답1 have already corroborated · 2 Have / hypothesized · 3 has been investigating / has investigated · 4 has refuted

문법 2. 현재완료진행

have/has been + -ing → 과거부터 지금까지 계속

I have been examining the data all morning.
It has been raining since dawn.

현재완료 vs 현재완료진행

  • 결과·경험·완료 → 현재완료
  • 지속·반복·미완료 강조 → 현재완료진행
She has written three books. (완료)
She has been writing all day. (계속)

연습 2

  1. We __________ (observe) the phenomenon since the experiment began.
  2. He looks exhausted. He __________ (work) on the thesis for hours.
  3. How long __________ you __________ (investigate) this case?
  4. I __________ (know) her since we collaborated on the project.
정답1 have been observing · 2 has been working · 3 have / been investigating · 4 have known

문법 3. 과거완료 · 시제 일치

had + p.p. = 과거보다 더 이전

과거완료진행: had been + -ing

When the audit started, officials had already verified the accounts.
By the time she arrived, they had been negotiating for hours.

시제 일치(간접화법·명사절):

주절 과거 → 종속절 과거·과거완료

He said he had finished the report.

연습 3

  1. After scientists __________ (formulate) the hypothesis, they designed trials.
  2. By 2020, the lab __________ (publish) over fifty papers.
  3. She explained that she __________ (not / receive) the notification.
  4. When we arrived, the committee __________ (debate) the issue for two hours.
정답1 had formulated · 2 had published · 3 had not received · 4 had been debating
