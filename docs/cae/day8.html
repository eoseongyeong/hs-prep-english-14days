문법 1. 고급 수동태 (get-passive / 이중수동)

get-passive: 구어체·상태 변화 강조

He got promoted last month.

이중수동 (double passive): to부정사 수동 뒤에 또 다른 수동

The suspect is expected to be charged tomorrow.
The proposal is believed to have been rejected.

감정/피해 강조 get-passive: get hurt, get caught, get fired

연습 1

  1. He __________ (get, fire) after the scandal broke.
  2. The bill __________ (expect, pass) next week.
  3. She __________ (get, promote) to senior manager last spring.
  4. The documents __________ (believe, have, destroy) before the audit.
정답1 got fired · 2 is expected to be passed · 3 got promoted · 4 are believed to have been destroyed

문법 2. 전치 강조 (Fronting for Emphasis)

목적어·보어를 문두로 이동해 강조

Such was the confusion that no one knew who was in charge.
A good leader she certainly is.
Never before had the team faced such pressure.

연습 2

  1. __________ (a remarkable teacher) he was, everyone agreed.
  2. __________ (such, be) the pressure that she nearly quit.
  3. __________ (never before) had the region seen such rainfall.
  4. __________ (this, argument) I simply cannot accept.
정답1 A remarkable teacher · 2 Such was · 3 Never before · 4 This argument

문법 3. 병렬구조 (Parallel Structure)

등위접속사·상관접속사로 연결된 요소는 문법적으로 동일한 형태

The plan is cost-effective, efficient, and sustainable.
She enjoys reading, writing, and editing.
Not only did he apologize but also offered compensation.

흔한 오류: to부정사와 동명사를 섞어 쓰는 것

연습 3

  1. The report is thorough, concise, and __________ (persuade).
  2. She likes hiking, swimming, and __________ (cycle).
  3. He not only missed the deadline but also __________ (fail) to notify anyone.
  4. The goal is to reduce costs and __________ (improve) efficiency.
정답1 persuasive · 2 cycling · 3 failed · 4 improve
