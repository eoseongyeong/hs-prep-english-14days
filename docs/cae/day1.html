단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. posit — (a) 가정하다, 상정하다 (b) 이분법 (c) 허울만 그럴듯한
  2. contest — (a) 이의를 제기하다, 다투다 (b) 거짓된, 가짜의 (c) 공리, 자명한 이치
  3. discredit — (a) 가정하다, 상정하다 (b) 신빙성을 떨어뜨리다 (c) 이의를 제기하다, 비난하다
  4. repudiate — (a) 공식적으로 부인하다, 거부하다 (b) 거짓된, 가짜의 (c) 이의를 제기하다, 비난하다

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. The defense __________ted every point raised by the prosecution. (반박하다)
  2. She refused to let anyone __________ her integrity. (이의를 제기하다)
  3. The findings __________ decades of conventional wisdom. (반박하다)

문법 1. 도치 강조구문 (Negative Inversion)

부정·제한 부사(구)가 문두에 오면 주어-동사 도치

부사(구)의미예
Never결코 ~않다Never had she seen such chaos.
Rarely/Seldom좀처럼 ~않다Rarely does the committee meet twice a month.
Not only...~뿐 아니라Not only did he resign, but he also apologized.
Little거의 ~않다Little did they know what awaited them.
No sooner...than~하자마자No sooner had she sat down than the phone rang.
Under no circumstances어떤 경우에도 ~않다Under no circumstances should you sign this.

도치 어순: 부정부사 + 조동사/do + 주어 + 동사원형

연습 1

  1. __________ (never) I seen such a compelling argument.
  2. __________ (rarely) the board question the chairman's decisions.
  3. No sooner __________ (the meeting begin) than the fire alarm went off.
  4. __________ (under no circumstances) you should reveal the source.
정답1 Never have · 2 Rarely does · 3 had begun · 4 Under no circumstances should

문법 2. 분열문 (Cleft Sentences)

It-cleft: It is/was + 강조 요소 + that/who ...

It was the manager who approved the budget.

Wh-cleft (What-cleft): What + S + V + is/was + 강조 요소

What surprised everyone was his sudden resignation.

All-cleft: All + S + V + is/was + to부정사

All she wants is to be heard.

연습 2

  1. __________ (it, be) the data, not the theory, that changed his mind.
  2. __________ (what, the report, reveal) was a pattern of neglect.
  3. It was in 2019 __________ the policy first came into effect.
  4. All he needed __________ (be) a little more time.
정답1 It was · 2 What the report revealed · 3 that · 4 was

문법 3. 명사화 (Nominalization)

동사/형용사 → 명사 전환으로 격식체 문장 완성

동사/형용사명사형예
decidedecisionThe committee's decision surprised everyone.
analyzeanalysisFurther analysis is required.
assumeassumptionThe assumption proved false.
significantsignificanceThe significance of the finding was clear.
awareawarenessPublic awareness has grown.

격식체 글쓰기에서는 동사절보다 명사구를 선호한다.

연습 3

  1. They decided to postpone the launch. → Their __________ to postpone the launch surprised investors.
  2. The data were analyzed carefully. → Careful __________ of the data was essential.
  3. He assumed that costs would fall. → His __________ that costs would fall proved false.
  4. The public became aware of the risks. → Growing __________ of the risks changed public opinion.
정답1 decision · 2 analysis · 3 assumption · 4 awareness
