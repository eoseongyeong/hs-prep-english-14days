문법 1. 조동사 + 완료 심화 (Modal Perfect)

조동사 + have + p.p.: 과거 사실에 대한 추측·후회·불필요

표현의미예
must have p.p.~했음이 틀림없다She must have left already.
can't/couldn't have p.p.~했을 리 없다He can't have said that.
should have p.p.~했어야 했다(안 함)You should have called first.
needn't have p.p.~할 필요 없었는데 했다You needn't have worried.
didn't need to~할 필요 없었다(중립)We didn't need to book in advance.

needn't have vs didn't need to 차이: needn't have는 "했지만 불필요했음", didn't need to는 단순 사실

연습 1

  1. She __________ (must, leave) already; her coat is gone.
  2. You __________ (needn't, worry) — the flight was delayed anyway.
  3. He __________ (can't, forget) the meeting; he mentioned it yesterday.
  4. We __________ (didn't need, bring) an umbrella since it never rained.
정답1 must have left · 2 needn't have worried · 3 can't have forgotten · 4 didn't need to bring

문법 2. 수동 보고구문 (Passive Reporting Structures)

It is + p.p. + that절 / S + is + p.p. + to부정사

구조예
It is said/believed/reported thatIt is believed that the CEO will resign.
S + is said/believed + to VThe CEO is believed to be resigning.
S + is said + to have p.p.She is said to have discovered the error.

신문 기사·학술 글에서 출처를 밝히지 않고 전달할 때 사용

연습 2

  1. __________ (it, report) that talks have collapsed.
  2. The suspect __________ (believe, flee) the country last week.
  3. __________ (it, say) that the policy will be reversed.
  4. The drug __________ (claim, reduce) symptoms significantly.
정답1 It is reported · 2 is believed to have fled · 3 It is said · 4 is claimed to reduce

문법 3. 사역동사 심화 (Causative have/get)

have/get + O + p.p./원형/to V

패턴의미예
have + O + p.p.(남이) O를 ~하게 하다(당하다)I had my car repaired.
get + O + p.p.(더 구어체)She got her hair cut.
have + O + 원형O가 직접 ~하게 시키다He had his assistant book the flight.
get + O + to VO가 ~하도록 설득/유도They got him to sign the contract.

연습 3

  1. I need to __________ (have, my laptop, fix) before the trip.
  2. She __________ (get, her son, clean) his room every Saturday.
  3. The manager __________ (have, the intern, prepare) the slides.
  4. It took some effort to __________ (get, them, agree) to the new terms.
정답1 have my laptop fixed · 2 gets her son to clean · 3 had the intern prepare · 4 get them to agree
