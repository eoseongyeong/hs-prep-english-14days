문법 1. 미래시제 뉘앙스 (be to / be about to)

공식적·예정된 미래

표현뉘앙스예
be to V공식 일정, 규정The president is to visit Berlin next week.
be about to V임박한 미래The plane is about to depart.
be on the point of -ing막 ~하려던 참She was on the point of leaving when he called.
be due to V예정되어 있다The report is due to be published Friday.

연습 1

  1. The minister __________ (be, to, announce) the results tomorrow morning.
  2. The train __________ (be, about, leave) — hurry!
  3. She __________ (be, on the point of, resign) when they offered a raise.
  4. The results __________ (be, due to) come out next Monday.
정답1 is to announce · 2 is about to leave · 3 was on the point of resigning · 4 are due to

문법 2. 완료진행 심화 (Perfect Progressive Nuance)

현재완료진행 vs 과거완료진행: 지속과 그 결과

She has been researching the topic for three years.
By the time he arrived, we had been waiting for two hours.

미래완료진행: will have been -ing

By June, she will have been working here for a decade.

연습 2

  1. They __________ (negotiate) for six hours by the time the deal was reached.
  2. By next year, he __________ (teach) at the university for twenty years.
  3. She __________ (already, work) there for a year when the merger happened.
  4. We __________ (wait) for over an hour when the bus finally arrived.
정답1 had been negotiating · 2 will have been teaching · 3 had already been working · 4 had been waiting

문법 3. 시간절 심화 (Time Clause Nuance)

시간 접속사 뉘앙스

접속사의미예
once일단 ~하면Once approved, the plan cannot be changed.
as soon as~하자마자She called as soon as she landed.
by the time~할 때쯤이면By the time you read this, I'll be gone.
the moment (that)~하는 순간The moment the doors opened, crowds rushed in.

시간 부사절에서는 미래 대신 현재시제 사용

연습 3

  1. __________ (once) the contract is signed, no changes can be made.
  2. She will call you the moment she __________ (arrive).
  3. By the time the show starts, most seats __________ (already, fill).
  4. __________ (as soon as) the results are announced, shares are expected to rise.
정답1 Once · 2 arrives · 3 will already have filled · 4 As soon as
