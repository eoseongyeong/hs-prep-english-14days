단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. cognition — (a) 인지 (b) 무기력한 (c) 내성, 자기성찰
  2. cognitive — (a) 대처하다 (b) 무기력한 (c) 인지의
  3. introspection — (a) 잠재의식(의) (b) 내성, 자기성찰 (c) 대처하다
  4. introspective — (a) 잠재의식(의) (b) 충동적인 (c) 내성적인, 자기성찰적인

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. Advertisers often appeal to the __________ desires of consumers. (잠재의식(의))
  2. Her __________ reaction was to protect the child. (본능적인)
  3. Trust your __________ when something feels wrong. (본능)
  4. He regretted the __________ decision almost immediately. (충동적인)
정답 보기A: 1-(a) 2-(c) 3-(b) 4-(c)
B: 5. subconscious · 6. instinctive · 7. instinct · 8. impulsive

문법 1. 한정사·수량사 심화 (a number of vs the number of)

a number of vs the number of

표현의미수 일치예
a number of + 복수명사많은 (수량)복수 동사A number of students were absent.
the number of + 복수명사~의 수(그 자체)단수 동사The number of applicants has risen.
a great deal of많은 (불가산)단수 동사A great deal of effort was required.
quite a few상당히 많은복수 동사Quite a few issues remain.

연습 1

  1. A number of proposals __________ (be) rejected outright.
  2. The number of complaints __________ (have) doubled this year.
  3. A great deal of research __________ (support) this conclusion.
  4. Quite a few employees __________ (express) concern about the change.
정답1 were · 2 has · 3 supports · 4 expressed

문법 2. 관사 고급 (Advanced Article Usage)

총칭·추상 표현에서의 관사

용법예
the + 형용사 = ~한 사람들the poor, the elderly, the unemployed
the + 단수명사 = 종 전체The whale is a mammal.
무관사 추상명사Justice must prevail. (일반 개념)
the + 추상명사 (특정)The justice of the ruling was questioned. (특정 사례)

연습 2

  1. __________ (the, elderly) often require additional social support.
  2. __________ (the, computer) has transformed modern communication.
  3. __________ (justice) is a universal ideal.
  4. __________ (the, silence) in the room was unbearable.
정답1 The elderly · 2 The computer · 3 Justice · 4 The silence

문법 3. 가산/불가산 뉘앙스

같은 단어라도 가산/불가산에 따라 의미가 달라짐

단어불가산가산
paper종이a paper = 논문
experience경험(전반)an experience = 특정 경험
light빛a light = 조명기구
room공간, 여지a room = 방
time시간(전반)a time = 특정 시기/횟수

연습 3

  1. She wrote __________ (a paper) on climate migration.
  2. Working abroad was __________ (an experience) she never forgot.
  3. There isn't much __________ (room) left in the schedule.
  4. We visited the museum several __________ (time) last year.
정답1 a paper · 2 an experience · 3 room · 4 times
