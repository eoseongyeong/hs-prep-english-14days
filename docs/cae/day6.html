15ambivalenta.양가적인She felt ambivalent about accepting the promotion.그녀는 승진을 받아들이는 것에 대해 양가적인 감정을 느꼈다.
16ambivalencen.양가감정His ambivalence toward the project frustrated his colleagues.그 프로젝트에 대한 그의 양가감정은 동료들을 답답하게 했다.

단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. exposition — (a) 주제의 설명, 해설 (b) 함축적 의미 (c) 교훈적인
  2. treatise — (a) 웅변적인, 설득력 있게 표현하는 (b) 응집성, 결속력 (c) 논문, 학술서
  3. polemic — (a) 함축적 의미 (b) 논쟁, 격론 (c) 장황한
  4. didactic — (a) 교훈적인 (b) 주제의 설명, 해설 (c) 과장법

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. The story can be read as an __________ critique of power. (우화적인)
  2. The magazine is known for its __________ take on politics. (풍자적인)
  3. Transitional phrases improve the __________ of an essay. (일관성)
  4. Repeated key terms help maintain textual __________. (응집성)
정답 보기A: 1-(a) 2-(c) 3-(b) 4-(a)
B: 5. allegorical · 6. satirical · 7. coherence · 8. cohesion

문법 1. 담화표지 (hence / thereby / whereas)

격식체 접속 부사

표현의미예
hence그러므로Costs rose; hence, prices increased.
thereby그렇게 함으로써He resigned, thereby avoiding a vote.
whereas반면에Sales rose, whereas profits fell.
thus따라서The data were flawed, thus invalid.
in that~라는 점에서The plan is risky in that it relies on one supplier.

연습 1

  1. Demand fell sharply; __________, production was cut.
  2. She signed the agreement, __________ committing the firm to new terms.
  3. The north saw growth, __________ the south experienced decline.
  4. The proposal is unusual __________ it challenges standard practice.
정답1 hence · 2 thereby · 3 whereas · 4 in that

문법 2. 완곡 표현 (Hedging Language)

단정을 피하고 가능성을 나타내는 표현

표현예
seem to / appear toThe theory seems to hold in most cases.
tend toYounger investors tend to take more risks.
arguablyThis is arguably the most important finding.
it could be argued thatIt could be argued that the policy failed.
to some extentThe claim is, to some extent, justified.

연습 2

  1. The results __________ (seem) to support the hypothesis.
  2. Smaller firms __________ (tend) to adapt more quickly.
  3. __________, this is the most significant reform in decades. (arguably)
  4. __________ that the reform came too late. (it could be argued)
정답1 seem · 2 tend · 3 Arguably · 4 It could be argued

문법 3. 전치사 연어 (Formal Preposition Collocations)

격식체에서 자주 쓰이는 전치사 연어

표현예
in light ofIn light of the evidence, the policy was revised.
with regard toLittle is known with regard to long-term effects.
in accordance withThe action was taken in accordance with the law.
at the expense ofGrowth came at the expense of the environment.
on the grounds thatThe appeal was rejected on the grounds that it lacked evidence.

연습 3

  1. __________ the new data, the committee revised its forecast.
  2. The report says little __________ implementation costs.
  3. The permit was issued __________ local regulations.
  4. Efficiency was achieved __________ quality.
정답1 In light of · 2 with regard to · 3 in accordance with · 4 at the expense of
