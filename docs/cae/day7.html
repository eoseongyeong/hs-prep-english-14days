문법 1. 화법 심화 (Reported Speech Nuance)

시제 일치의 예외: 불변의 진리·현재도 유효한 사실은 시제 유지

She said the Earth revolves around the Sun.
He mentioned that he is moving to Berlin next month.

보고동사 + 목적어 + to V / -ing / that절

동사패턴예
insist on-ingHe insisted on paying the bill.
deny-ingShe denied taking the file.
accuse A of-ingThey accused him of lying.
warn A not toto VShe warned them not to leave.
urge A toto VHe urged the board to reconsider.

연습 1

  1. He said that water __________ (boil) at 100°C.
  2. She denied __________ (take) the missing files.
  3. They accused the firm __________ (mislead) investors.
  4. The officer warned drivers __________ (not, exceed) the speed limit.
정답1 boils · 2 taking · 3 of misleading · 4 not to exceed

문법 2. 격식체 구동사 (Formal Phrasal Verbs)

구동사격식체 동의어예
put offpostponeThe meeting was postponed.
bring aboutcauseThe policy brought about/caused major change.
come acrossencounterResearchers came across/encountered an anomaly.
go on withcontinueThe talks continued/went on.
put up withtolerateResidents tolerated/put up with the noise.
look intoinvestigateOfficials investigated/looked into the claim.

  1. The launch was __________ (put off) due to technical issues.
  2. Rapid urbanization __________ (bring about) significant environmental change.
  3. During the audit, they __________ (come across) several discrepancies.
  4. The agency agreed to __________ (look into) the complaint.
정답1 put off · 2 brought about · 3 came across · 4 look into
