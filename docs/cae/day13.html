문법 1. 부가의문·간접의문 격식체

격식체 부가의문 및 간접의문

You wouldn't happen to know the answer, would you?
I wonder whether the proposal will be approved.
Could you tell me what time the meeting starts?

간접의문 어순: 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (도치 없음)

연습 1

  1. Could you tell me __________ (where, the office, be)?
  2. I wonder __________ (whether, they, agree) to the terms.
  3. You haven't seen my keys, __________?
  4. Do you know __________ (why, the flight, be delay)?
정답1 where the office is · 2 whether they will agree · 3 have you · 4 why the flight was delayed

문법 2. what/all 강조구문

What절/All절을 이용한 강조

What the company needs is stronger leadership.
All that matters is the final result.
What worries investors most is the lack of transparency.

연습 2

  1. __________ (what, the team, need) is more time.
  2. __________ (all, she, want) is a fair hearing.
  3. __________ (what, surprise, everyone) was the sudden announcement.
  4. __________ (all, that, matter) now is customer satisfaction.
정답1 What the team needs · 2 All she wants · 3 What surprised everyone · 4 All that matters

문법 3. 부정어 도치 심화 (Only + 부사절)

Only + 부사(구)/절 도치

Only after the trial did the truth emerge.
Only when prices fell did demand recover.
Not until she apologized did he respond.

Not until = ~하고 나서야 비로소

연습 3

  1. __________ (only after, the audit) did the fraud come to light.
  2. __________ (only when, costs fall) will demand increase.
  3. __________ (not until, she explain) did the misunderstanding end.
  4. __________ (only then, they, realize) their mistake.
정답1 Only after the audit · 2 Only when costs fall · 3 Not until she explained · 4 Only then did they realize
