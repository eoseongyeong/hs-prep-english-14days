문법 1. 전치사 관계절 (whereby / wherein)

whereby = by which, wherein = in which (격식체 관계부사)

표현의미예
whereby그것에 의하여a system whereby citizens vote online
wherein그 안에서a report wherein the flaws are detailed
whereupon그러자, 그 후에He apologized, whereupon she relented.

whereby/wherein은 사물 선행사 + 전치사+관계대명사 결합 형태

연습 1

  1. They devised a scheme __________ profits are shared equally.
  2. The contract contains a clause __________ either party may withdraw.
  3. He revealed the truth, __________ the room fell silent.
  4. This is the process __________ raw data becomes usable information.
정답1 whereby · 2 wherein · 3 whereupon · 4 whereby

문법 2. 강조 도치 (so...that / 강조 do)

So + 형용사/부사 + that절 도치

So severe was the damage that the building had to be demolished.

Such + be동사 + that절 도치

Such was her influence that no one dared object.

강조 do/does/did + 동사원형

She does understand the risks. / He did warn us.

연습 2

  1. __________ (so, great, be) the demand that the store sold out in an hour.
  2. __________ (such, be) his reputation that clients came from abroad.
  3. I know it seems unlikely, but she __________ (강조, pass) the exam.
  4. He claims he never received the email, but we __________ (강조, send) it twice.
정답1 So great was · 2 Such was · 3 does · did · 4 did send

문법 3. the 비교급 ~, the 비교급 … (Double Comparatives)

the + 비교급 ~, the + 비교급 …: ~할수록 더 …하다

The more carefully you plan, the fewer mistakes you make.
The longer the delay, the higher the cost.
The sooner we start, the sooner we finish.

연습 3

  1. __________ you practice, __________ you become. (더 많이 연습할수록 더 능숙해진다)
  2. __________ the risk, __________ the potential reward.
  3. __________ people join, __________ the event becomes.
  4. __________ she explained, __________ confused he got.
정답1 The more / the more skilled · 2 The greater / the greater · 3 The more / the more lively · 4 The more / the more
