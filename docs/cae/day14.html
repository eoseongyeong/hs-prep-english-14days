단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. ubiquitous — (a) 근거 없는 (b) 불필요한, 여분의 (c) 어디에나 있는
  2. quintessential — (a) 어디에나 있는 (b) 신중한, 조심스러운 (c) 전형적인, 정수의
  3. unequivocal — (a) 명백한, 모호하지 않은 (b) 어디에나 있는 (c) 불필요한, 무자비한
  4. ostensible — (a) 터무니없는 (b) 변덕스러운, 불안정한 (c) 표면상의

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. He resigned, __________ for personal reasons. (표면상으로는)
  2. She remained an __________ campaigner for reform. (지칠 줄 모르는)
  3. The __________ host kept guests entertained for hours. (수다스러운)
  4. The report seemed designed to __________ rather than clarify the issue. (애매하게 하다)
정답 보기A: 1-(c) 2-(c) 3-(a) 4-(c)
B: 5. ostensibly · 6. indefatigable · 7. garrulous · 8. obfuscate

문법 1. 격식/비격식 전환 (Register Transformation)

같은 의미, 다른 격식 수준

비격식격식

a lot ofa great deal of / numerous
get betterimprove

buthowever / nevertheless

CAE Reading & Use of English Part 4(문장 바꿔쓰기)의 핵심 전략

연습 1

  1. We need to find out why sales dropped. → We need to __________ why sales dropped.
  2. A lot of people attended the event. → __________ people attended the event.
  3. The economy got better after the reform. → The economy __________ after the reform.
  4. The flight was delayed because of the storm. → The flight was delayed __________ the storm.
정답1 ascertain/determine · 2 A great deal of / Numerous · 3 improved · 4 owing to/due to

문법 2. 요약문 작성법 (Summary Writing)

요약 작성 원칙

1. 핵심 논지만 남기고 예시·부연은 제외

2. 원문 표현을 자신의 말로 바꿔쓰기 (paraphrase)

3. 한 문장 안에 여러 내용을 압축 (분사구문, 관계절 활용)

4. 지나치게 긴 인용 대신 요약된 명사구 사용

원문: "The company faced financial difficulties, so it decided to lay off a third of its staff."
요약: Facing financial difficulties, the company cut a third of its workforce.

연습 2

  1. 원문: The rules were unclear, so many people broke them unintentionally. → 요약: __________ unclear, many broke them unintentionally.
  2. 원문: She was tired, but she kept working. → 요약: __________ tired, she kept working.
  3. 원문: The report is long. It covers ten topics. → 요약: The __________ report covers ten topics.
  4. 핵심만 남기기: 'The committee met for three hours and eventually, after much debate, approved the plan.' → __________ approved the plan after lengthy debate.
정답1 Being · 2 Despite being · 3 lengthy/lengthy ten-topic · 4 The committee

연습 3

  1. [도치] __________ had the results been announced when shares surged.
  2. [분열문] __________ the CEO's resignation that shocked investors most.
  3. [혼합가정] If the board __________ (act) sooner, the crisis would be over by now.
  4. [수동보고] The merger __________ (believe, finalize) by next quarter.
  5. [전치사관계절] This is the mechanism __________ funds are redistributed.
  6. [영작] 그 정책은 논란의 여지가 있지만, 결국 시행되었다. (albeit 사용)
  7. [영작] 그녀가 사임하고 나서야 진실이 드러났다. (Not until 도치)
  8. [요약] 'Because the evidence was inconclusive, the case was dropped.' → __________ evidence, the case was dropped.
