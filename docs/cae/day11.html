A. 뜻 고르기

  1. aesthetic — (a) 미학(적인) (b) 소박한, 검소한 (c) 추상적인; 추상
  2. aesthetics — (a) 시대를 초월한 (b) 미학 (c) 초현실적인
  3. avant-garde — (a) 복제품 (b) 위조(품) (c) 전위적인; 전위 예술
  4. eclectic — (a) 진정성, 진품성 (b) 추상적인; 추상 (c) 다양한 요소를 모은, 절충적인

  1. The cathedral's ceiling is covered in __________ carvings. (화려하게 장식된)

문법 1. 접속부사 (nonetheless / notwithstanding)

문장 간 연결하는 격식체 부사

표현의미예
nonetheless/nevertheless그럼에도 불구하고The risks were high; nonetheless, they proceeded.
notwithstanding~에도 불구하고(전치사)Notwithstanding the risks, they proceeded.
that said그렇긴 하지만It's expensive. That said, it works well.
conversely반대로Prices rose in the north; conversely, they fell in the south.

연습 1

  1. The evidence was weak; __________, the jury convicted.
  2. __________ the objections, the board approved the merger.
  3. The product is costly. __________, it has sold well.
  4. Urban areas saw growth; __________, rural areas declined.
정답1 nonetheless · 2 Notwithstanding · 3 That said · 4 conversely

문법 2. 양보·대조절 심화

양보(인정) vs 대조(단순 차이) 구분

접속사기능예
although/even though양보 (인정하지만)Although it rained, we went out.
whereas/while대조 (단순 비교)Sales rose, whereas costs fell.
however + 형용사/부사아무리 ~해도However difficult it seems, we must try.
much as~이긴 하지만Much as I admire him, I disagree here.

연습 2

  1. __________ he apologized repeatedly, she remained upset.
  2. City incomes rose, __________ rural incomes stagnated.
  3. __________ hard the task may be, giving up isn't an option.
  4. __________ I respect her opinion, I can't agree this time.
정답1 Although · 2 whereas · 3 However · 4 Much as

문법 3. 복문 결합 (Combining Clauses)

여러 절을 하나의 복문으로 결합하는 전략

Because the data were incomplete, and because time was short, the team postponed the study.
→ Given that the data were incomplete and time was short, the team postponed the study.

Given that / Considering that / In view of the fact that = ~을 고려할 때

연습 3

  1. __________ (given that) the budget is limited, the scope must be reduced.
  2. __________ (considering that) she is new, her performance is impressive.
  3. __________ (in view of the fact that) profits fell, layoffs were announced.
  4. Combine: The plan is bold. It is risky. → The plan is __________ bold __________ risky.
정답1 Given that · 2 Considering that · 3 In view of the fact that · 4 both / and
