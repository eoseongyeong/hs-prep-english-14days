단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. tentative — (a) 정성적인 (b) 잠정적인 (c) 종단적인
  2. provisional — (a) 결정적인, 최종적인 (b) 타고난, 선천적인 (c) 임시의, 잠정적인
  3. preliminary — (a) 예비의 (b) 반증 가능한 (c) 재현 가능성
  4. definitive — (a) 결정적인, 최종적인 (b) 탄탄한, 견고한 (c) 정황적인

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. The report identifies the most __________ risks facing the industry. (두드러진)
  2. Please keep your comments __________ to the topic under discussion. (관련 있는)
  3. Researchers tried to eliminate __________ variables from the experiment. (무관한)
  4. The study found no __________ link between the two factors. (인과관계의)
정답 보기A: 1-(b) 2-(c) 3-(a) 4-(a)
B: 5. salient · 6. germane · 7. extraneous · 8. causal

문법 1. 복합 분사구문 (완료·수동 분사)

Having + p.p.: 주절보다 먼저 일어난 일

Having finished the report, she left early.

Being + p.p. / p.p. (수동 분사구문)

(Being) Written in haste, the report contained several errors.

부정형: Not + 분사

Not having received a reply, he called again.

연습 1

  1. __________ (finish) the exam early, she reviewed her answers twice.
  2. __________ (write) in the 1800s, the novel still feels relevant today.
  3. __________ (not receive) any confirmation, they assumed the deal was off.
  4. __________ (exhaust) by the journey, the team decided to rest.
정답1 Having finished · 2 Written · 3 Not having received · 4 Exhausted

문법 2. 독립분사구문 (Absolute Construction)

주절과 주어가 다를 때 분사구문 앞에 의미상 주어를 남긴다

The weather permitting, we will hold the event outdoors.
All things considered, the plan was a success.
Weather permitting / Everything else being equal — 자주 쓰이는 관용 표현

연습 2

  1. __________ (the deadline, approach), the team worked through the night.
  2. All things __________ (consider), the merger was a wise decision.
  3. __________ (there, be) no objections, the motion was passed.
  4. __________ (weather, permit), the launch will proceed as planned.
정답1 The deadline approaching · 2 considered · 3 There being · 4 Weather permitting

문법 3. 생략과 대용 (Ellipsis & Substitution)

반복되는 요소는 생략하거나 so/do so/one 등으로 대체

대용 표현용법예
so / notthink, hope, believe 뒤"Will it rain?" "I think so."
do so앞의 동사구 대체He asked her to leave, and she did so.
one(s)앞의 단수/복수 명사 대체I need a pen; do you have one?
(생략)반복되는 절 생략She can swim, and he can (swim), too.

연습 3

  1. "Is the project finished?" "I believe __________." (so 사용)
  2. He was told to leave the room, and he __________ (do so) without protest.
  3. I lost my umbrella, so I need to buy a new __________ (대명사).
  4. She wanted to resign, and eventually she __________ (반복 생략).
정답1 so · 2 did so · 3 one · 4 did
