A. 뜻 고르기

  1. pedagogy — (a) 교수법, 교육학 (b) 직업(교육)의 (c) 종신 재직권
  2. pedagogical — (a) 교육학적인 (b) 수리력 (c) 박식함
  3. curriculum — (a) 교육과정 (b) 표절하다 (c) 독학자
  4. syllabus — (a) 강의계획서 (b) 학위논문 (c) 독학자

문법 1. 동사 패턴 의미차 (stop to do vs stop doing)

to부정사 vs 동명사: 의미가 달라지는 동사

동사+ to V+ -ing
stop하기 위해 멈추다~하는 것을 그만두다
remember~할 것을 기억하다(미래)~했던 것을 기억하다(과거)
forget~할 것을 잊다~했던 것을 잊다
try시도하다(노력)시험 삼아 해보다
regret~하게 되어 유감이다~한 것을 후회하다

연습 1

  1. He stopped __________ (smoke) five years ago.
  2. She stopped __________ (check) her phone during the meeting.
  3. I remember __________ (lock) the door before we left.
  4. We regret __________ (inform) you that your application was unsuccessful.
정답1 smoking · 2 to check · 3 locking · 4 to inform

문법 2. 형용사+전치사 연어

형용사 + 전치사 고정 결합

형용사전치사예
skepticalof/aboutShe is skeptical of the claim.
indifferenttoHe seemed indifferent to the outcome.
conducivetoA quiet room is conducive to studying.
synonymouswithSuccess became synonymous with hard work.
oblivioustoHe was oblivious to the danger.

연습 2

  1. Many economists remain skeptical __________ the forecast.
  2. The manager seemed indifferent __________ staff concerns.
  3. A calm environment is conducive __________ concentration.
  4. He was completely oblivious __________ the risks involved.
정답1 of/about · 2 to · 3 to · 4 to

문법 3. 동사+전치사 연어

동사 + 전치사 고정 결합

동사전치사예
account for~을 설명하다/차지하다Exports account for half of GDP.
dispose of~을 처분하다The firm disposed of its old equipment.
refrain from~을 삼가다Please refrain from smoking.
embark on~에 착수하다The company embarked on a new venture.
capitalize on~을 활용하다Investors capitalized on the price drop.

연습 3

  1. Rising fuel costs account __________ much of the price increase.
  2. The company disposed __________ its outdated machinery.
  3. Please refrain __________ using phones during the ceremony.
  4. The startup embarked __________ an ambitious expansion plan.
정답1 for · 2 of · 3 from · 4 on
