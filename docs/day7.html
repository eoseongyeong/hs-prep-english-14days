단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. avow — (a) 인용·언급하다 (b) 공언하다 (c) 발언하다
  2. cite — (a) 암시·비난하다 (b) 언급하다; 발언 (c) 인용·언급하다
  3. confide — (a) 외치다 (b) 주장· 의도하다 (c) 털어놓다
  4. contend — (a) 주장·경쟁하다 (b) 열거하다 (c) 통지하다

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. The court __________d the law unconstitutional. (선언하다)
  2. The site __________s liability for errors. (부인)
  3. Employees must not __________ trade secrets. (누설하다)
  4. She __________ed that the result was impossible. (외치다)
정답 보기A: 1-(b) 2-(c) 3-(c) 4-(a)
B: 5. declare · 6. disclaim · 7. divulge · 8. exclaim

문법 1. 간접화법 시제·부사 변화

직접 → 간접: say/tell + (that) + 시제 후진

직접간접
am/is/arewas/were
do/doesdid
will/can/maywould/could/might
have/hashad
am/is/are doingwas/were doing
have p.p.had p.p.

시간·장소 부사: now→then, today→that day, tomorrow→the next/following day, yesterday→the day before, here→there, this→that

연습 1

  1. “I **will disclose** the findings tomorrow,” she said. → She said she __________ the findings the next day.
  2. “We **are negotiating** now,” they replied. → They replied they __________ then.
  3. “I **have never witnessed** such fraud,” he testified. → He testified he __________ such fraud.
  4. “The policy **was enacted** last year,” the memo **purports**. → The memo purports the policy __________ the previous year.
정답1 would disclose · 2 were negotiating · 3 had never witnessed · 4 had been enacted

문법 2. 간접의문문

의문문 → 평서문 어순 (의문사 + 주어 + 동사)

Do you know where he lives?
I wonder whether the court will uphold the ruling.
Tell me what time the session adjourns.

간접의문에서 do/does/did 제거

What did he say? → what he said
Where does she work? → where she works

연습 2

  1. “Where does the evidence originate?” → The judge asked where __________ .
  2. “Has the witness testified?” → I asked whether the witness __________ .
  3. “Why were they forewarned?” → Do you know why __________ ?
  4. “When will the board convene?” → Please notify me when __________ .
정답1 the evidence originated / originates · 2 had testified · 3 they had been forewarned / they were forewarned · 4 the board would convene

문법 3. 명령·제안·요청 전달

유형패턴
명령tell/order + O + to V / not to V
요청ask/request + O + to V
제안suggest + -ing / suggest + that + (should)
금지warn + O + not to V / forbid + O + to V
“Submit the report.” → He told me to submit the report.
“Don't divulge secrets.” → She warned us not to divulge secrets.
“Let's convene early.” → He suggested convening early.

연습 3

  1. “Please refrain from speculation.” → The chair __________ us __________ from speculation.
  2. “Broach the topic carefully.” → She advised him __________ the topic carefully.
  3. “Let's petition for reform.” → They suggested __________ for reform.
  4. “Do not mislead the public.” → Officials warned journalists __________ the public.
정답1 asked / to refrain · 2 to broach · 3 petitioning · 4 not to mislead
