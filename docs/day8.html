문법 1. 관계부사 (where / when / why / how)

관계부사선행사= 전치사+which
whereplacein/at which
whentimeon/in which
whyreasonfor which
howway/methodin which (the way how ❌)
This is the region where migrants sojourn temporarily.
I remember the day when the treaty was abrogated.
Tell me the reason why they evacuated the city.

연습 1

  1. That is the village __________ he __________ (exile) for decades.
  2. Spring is the season __________ birds __________ (migrate) north.
  3. Do you know the reason __________ they __________ (relocate) headquarters?
  4. This is __________ researchers __________ (excavate) ancient artifacts.
정답1 where / was exiled · 2 when / migrate · 3 why / relocated · 4 where / excavate

문법 2. whose · of which · of whom

whose = ~의 (사람·사물)

a nation whose borders demarcate ethnic divisions

of which / of whom (격식·사물)

the treaty, the terms of which were disputed
three scholars, one of whom sojourned abroad

a/an/the + N + of which/whom · some/many/few + of which/whom

연습 2

  1. They inhabit a region __________ water resources __________ (permeate) every valley.
  2. The report cited three studies, the conclusions __________ __________ (support) our view.
  3. She met diplomats __________ credentials __________ (verify) by the embassy.
  4. We visited ten sites, two __________ __________ (excavate) last summer.
정답1 whose / permeate · 2 of which / supported · 3 whose / were verified · 4 of which / were excavated

문법 3. 제한적 · 계속적 · which절 전체

제한적(콤마 ❌): 선행사 한정

Students who adhere to guidelines succeed.

계속적(콤마 ⭕): 부가 정보, that ❌

Seoul, which hosts global summits, attracts millions.

which = 앞 절 전체

The treaty was abrogated, which provoked international criticism.
(= and this provoked …)

  2. The policy was enacted, __________ immediately __________ (provoke) debate.
  3. He failed to vacate the premises, __________ __________ (lead) to eviction.
  4. Scientists __________ research intersects with ours will collaborate.
정답1 N · 2 which / provoked · 3 which / led · 4 whose
