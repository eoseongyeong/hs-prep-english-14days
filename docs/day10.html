A. 뜻 고르기

  1. agitate — (a) 위축시키다 (b) 전율· electrify (c) 동요· 선동하다
  2. bewilder — (a) 당황케 하다 (b) 짜증· 괴롭히다 (c) 매혹하다
  3. captivate — (a) 당혹스럽게 하다 (b) 동요· 선동하다 (c) 매료시키다
  4. daunt — (a) 당혹스럽게 하다 (b) 경외감을 주다 (c) 위축시키다

문법 1. 감정분사 쌍 (-ing vs -ed)

-ing (능동·일으킴)-ed (수동·느낌)
a bewildering puzzlea bewildered student
an exasperating delayexasperated commuters
a captivating speechcaptivated listeners

동사 → 분사 형용사: amaze→amazing/amazed · perplex→perplexing/perplexed

The mesmerizing performance left us mesmerized.

연습 1

  1. The __________ (perplex) instructions left users __________ (perplex).
  2. An __________ (electrify) goal sent the crowd into __________ (electrify) cheers.
  3. She felt __________ (mortify) after the __________ (mortify) error.
  4. The __________ (fascinate) lecture kept students __________ (fascinate).
정답1 perplexing / perplexed · 2 electrifying / electrified · 3 mortified / mortifying · 4 fascinating / fascinated

문법 2. 분사구문 · 완료분사구문

분사구문 = 접속사+주어 생략, 부사적 역할

Having finished the audit, they submitted the report.
Not knowing the rules, he violated protocol.
Written in plain language, the law is accessible.

완료분사구문: Having + p.p. (앞 절보다 먼저)

Having been warned, residents evacuated.

연습 2

  1. __________ (Agitate) by the news, investors sold shares.
  2. __________ (not / anticipate) the backlash, the firm apologized.
  3. __________ (Examine) thoroughly, the evidence was deemed insufficient.
  4. __________ (Complete) the survey, researchers analyzed the data.
정답1 Agitated · 2 Not having anticipated · 3 (When) Examined / Having been examined · 4 Having completed

문법 3. with + 명사 + 분사 · 독립분사구문

with + O + -ing/p.p./adj/prep

He listened with his eyes closed.
She walked with her assistant following her.

독립분사구문 (주어 다를 때)

The weather being fine, we continued the excavation.
All things considered, the policy succeeded.

연습 3

  1. He read the verdict with his hands __________ (tremble).
  2. She entered with reporters __________ (besiege) the entrance.
  3. __________ (Time / be) limited, we prioritized key questions.
  4. The treaty was signed with both nations __________ (satisfy).
정답1 trembling · 2 besieging · 3 Time being · 4 satisfied
