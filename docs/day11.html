단어 확인 퀴즈

A. 뜻 고르기

  1. accentuate — (a) 의미하다 (b) 강조· highlight (c) 강조· 부각
  2. allude — (a) 전형· epitomize (b) 암시· 언급 (c) 강조· stress
  3. delineate — (a) 윤곽· delineate (b) 요약하다 (c) 의미하다
  4. depict — (a) 묘사하다 (b) encapsulate (c) 강조· 부각

B. 빈칸 채우기

  1. Footnotes __________ obscure terms. (명확히 하다)
  2. The slogan __________s the campaign. (encapsulate)
  3. She __________s professional integrity. (전형)
  4. Case studies __________ key concepts. (전형)
정답 보기A: 1-(c) 2-(b) 3-(a) 4-(a)
B: 5. elucidate · 6. encapsulate · 7. epitomize · 8. exemplify

문법 1. 명사절 (that / whether / wh-)

명사절 = 주어·목적어·보어 역할

That inequality persists worries economists. (주어)
The data indicate that reforms work. (목적어)
The question is whether we should intervene. (보어)

wh-절: what / who / where / why / how · 평서 어순

I don't understand why he resigned.

연습 1

  1. __________ the committee will convene remains uncertain.
  2. The report clarifies __________ caused the breach.
  3. Experts disagree on __________ the policy should be amended.
  4. It is obvious __________ he __________ (distort) the facts.
정답1 Whether / When · 2 what · 3 whether · 4 that / distorted

문법 2. It 가주어 · It 강조 구문

It + be + adj/n + to V / that절 / wh- to V

It is crucial to clarify assumptions.
It surprised us that sales declined.

It is/was + 강조부분 + that/who + …

It was in 2020 that the law was enacted.
It is through evidence that claims are vindicated.

연습 2

  1. __________ is essential to annotate sources accurately.
  2. __________ appears that the assertion lacks support.
  3. It was the witness __________ __________ (testify) that changed the trial.
  4. It is by scrutinizing data __________ scientists __________ (refute) errors.
정답1 It · 2 It · 3 who / testified · 4 that / refute

문법 3. 부정어 도치

부정·제한 부사(구) 문두 → 조동사/be + 주어

부정어예
never, seldom, rarelyNever have I witnessed such bias.
not onlyNot only did she summarize, but she also critiqued.
only + 부사구Only then did I realize the flaw.
littleLittle did he know the consequences.
no soonerNo sooner had I left than it rained.

So/Neither 도치: So do I. / Neither can she.

연습 3

  1. I have never seen such rhetoric. → Never __________ I __________ such rhetoric.
  2. She did not only outline the thesis. → Not only __________ she __________ the thesis.
  3. I cannot affirm the claim. → Neither __________ I __________ the claim.
  4. Hardly had the session begun when protesters __________ (accost) delegates.
정답1 have / seen · 2 did / outline · 3 can / affirm · 4 accosted
